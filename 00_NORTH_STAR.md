### A3. Threads to track across all theory readings

For every reading, the note should answer the same five questions. This makes the final notes comparable.

1. What is "integration" for this author (process, end state, or both)?
2. What drives it (spillover, elites, transactions, security, markets, interdependence)?
3. What are the actors (states, supranational bodies, interest groups, communities)?
4. How is it measured or tested?
5. Which author does this one build on or argue against?

Suggested clusters for the final notes:

| Cluster | Readings | Core idea |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Functionalism | Mitrany | Cooperation on technical tasks builds peace |
| Neo-functionalism | Haas, Schmitter, Nye, Lindberg, Barrera and Haas | Spillover pushes integration forward |
| Transactionalism | Deutsch et al., Puchala, Etzioni | Communication and community drive integration |
| Economic integration | Balassa | Stages from free trade area to full union |
| Critique and measurement | Hansen, Nye, Galtung, Lindberg | Can integration be defined and measured? |
| Institutions and governance | Stone Sweet and Sandholtz, Gehring, Mattli | Supranational rules and regimes |
| Crisis and new regionalism | Schimmelfennig, Warleigh-Lack, Soderbaum, Borzel and Risse, Gibb | Beyond Europe, beyond old theory |
| Collective security | Clark, Tarzi | Limits of security-based cooperation |
