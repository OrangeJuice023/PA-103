# Reading 25 — Barrera, Mario, and Ernst B. Haas (1969)

**Citation (WEB, accessed 2026-10-27):**
Barrera, Mario Etchechury, and Ernst B. Haas. 1969. "The Operationalization of Some Variables Related to Regional Integration: A Research Note." *International Organization* 23(1): 150-160. DOI: 10.1017/s0020818300025595.
- https://doi.org/10.1017/s0020818300025595
- https://ideas.repec.org/a/cup/intorg/v23y1969i01p150-160_01.html

**Status:** WEB — outside research, not from class slides. Do not present as professor content.

**1. What is "integration"?**
Treated as an empirically measurable phenomenon: regional integration broken into variables that can be operationalized and observed (a process to be measured, not assumed).

**2. What drives it?**
The variables related to regional integration (transaction flows, institutional authority, etc.) — the note is about how to measure them rather than proposing a single driver.

**3. What are the actors?**
States and regional institutions/organs whose authority can be observed and coded.

**4. How is it measured or tested?**
Operationalization: defining observable indicators for integration-related variables so they can be coded and tested empirically. A "research note" on measurement method.

**5. Builds on / argues against?**
Builds on Haas's earlier integration work (including *The Study of Regional Integration*, IO 1958/1970). Argues against purely pre-theoretical or purely speculative integration theory; calls for empirical operationalization.

**Cluster:** Critique and measurement / Neo-functionalism.
