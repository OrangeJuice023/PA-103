**1. What is "integration"?**
A process: the integration of states into larger unions without the use of force; a continuous "federalizing process" in which economic integration may be a first step toward political unity.

**2. What drives it?**
Economic integration as a catalyst; the question is whether/under what conditions economic integration automatically triggers political unity. Focus on patterns and catalysts across regions.

**3. What are the actors?**
States/nations being integrated into larger unions.

**4. How is it measured or tested?**
Comparative analysis of patterns across regions (ties to Nye's "Comparative Regional Integration: Concept and Measurement," IO 22(4), 1968); inquiry into conditions/catalysts rather than one metric.

**5. Builds on / argues against?**
Builds on neo-functionalism (Haas). Argues against the assumption that economic integration automatically and inevitably produces political unity — integration is conditional, not automatic.

**Cluster:** Critique and measurement / Neo-functionalism.
